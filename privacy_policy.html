Privacy Policy

App Name: Noor: Prayer Times & Quran | Package ID: com.noor.islamic.app

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

Privacy Commitment: Noor is a privacy-first Islamic application. We do NOT collect, sell, monetize, or track your personal information or historical location on any servers.

1. Overview

Welcome to Noor ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data handling when you use the Noor mobile application.

2. Information Accessed & How It Is Used

A. Location Information (Approximate & Precise)

We access your device's geographical coordinates (latitude & longitude) solely to:

  • Calculate exact Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) for your local area.
  • Compute the exact Qibla direction angle and distance to the Holy Kaaba in Makkah.

Note: Coordinates are processed ephemerally on-device or via secure HTTPS API calls. We do not store or track your location history.

B. Device Orientation Sensors

We utilize the device gyroscope, accelerometer, and magnetometer sensors to provide a smooth real-time 360° Qibla compass needle. Sensor data is processed in live memory and never logged or stored.

C. Notifications & Background Service

Used strictly to deliver timely prayer alarms and live countdowns in the notification tray so alarms trigger reliably without being terminated by device battery optimizations.

D. Local Storage

User settings, selected reciters, Dhikr Tasbih history counts, and saved Hadith bookmarks are stored purely on your physical device.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app connects to public, reputable Islamic data sources over secure encrypted connections (HTTPS):

  • AlAdhan API: For prayer timetable and Hijri calendar data.
  • Quran Audio CDNs: For streaming audio recitations.

4. Children's Privacy

Noor is designed for general audiences and contains no mature content. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

5. Data Deletion & Control

You have full control over your data. You can erase all bookmarks, history, and cached data at any time via in-app Settings or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.
